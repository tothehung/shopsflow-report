# Overview of Internship & Technical Event Series (FCAJ Events)

During my internship and study of the **Cloud Computing Architecture (AWS Academy)** course at Swinburne Vietnam, I had the privilege of participating in a series of 3 specialized technical events organized under the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program. These events were jointly hosted by **Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam**, leading technology solution partners (*Cloud Kinetics*, *Renova Cloud*, *Styl Solutions*, *Endava*), and the *AWS Study Group* community.

This series provided comprehensive real-world perspectives, spanning Enterprise Architecture design principles, Artificial Intelligence integration (Generative AI, AI Agents, DevSecOps), deep Observability mindsets, and hands-on lessons from a 24-hour continuous Hackathon competition.

### 1. [Event 1: AWS Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application](4.1-Event1/)

* **Date & Time:** 09:00 – 12:00, **July 4, 2026**
* **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City.
* **Speakers:** Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung (*Head of SA, AWS VN*), Mr. Banh Cam Vinh (*Data Engineer, Renova Cloud*), Ms. Nhu Tran (*Account Manager, AWS VN*), Mr. Khang Nguyen (*SA, Cloud Kinetics*).
* **Core Takeaways:**
  * **Industry-Based Recruitment Shift:** Transitioning from purely technical roles toward a deep business domain understanding across 6 core economic verticals (Fintech, E-commerce, Manufacturing, Real Estate, Consumer/Health, Emerging).
  * **Data Platform DNA Framework:** Standardizing 5-layer enterprise data architectures (*Ingestion, Processing, Storage, Governance & Catalog, Analytics & Serving*).
  * **School vs. Production Reality Gap:** Analyzing the contrast between classroom assignments and production pressures (dirty data, tight deadlines, financial risks).
  * **Architecture Mindset & Ownership:** Emphasizing *Ownership*, treating communication as a technical skill (*Communication as a Technical Skill*), and the career value formula: $\text{Result} = \text{Capability} \times \text{Visibility} \times \text{Consistency}$.

### Key Value & Accumulated Skills Summary

Through this 3-event series, I have solidified 3 essential knowledge pillars shaping my engineering career:

1. **Technical & Architectural Excellence:**
   - Mastered Multi-AZ Enterprise architecture patterns, Data Platform DNA, and Serverless / Multi-Agent design principles.
   - Gained hands-on exposure to cutting-edge AI tools (Amazon Bedrock, AWS Security Agent, Amazon Q Developer) for development and security automation.

2. **Business-First & User-Centric Mindset:**
   - Always start from business pain points and cost constraints (*ROI / Cost Efficiency*) rather than chasing tools for their own sake.
   - Shifted operational mindsets to **Observability**, safeguarding user experience while enforcing Human-in-the-Loop governance.

3. **Career Development & Professional Soft Skills:**
   - Cultivated personal responsibility (*Ownership*), pressure resilience (*Resilience*), and technical communication (*Communication as a Technical Skill*).
   - Actively expanded professional networks (*Networking*), maintained hands-on practice (*Hands-on*), and committed to a culture of lifelong learning (*Lifelong Learning*).
