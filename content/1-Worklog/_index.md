- **Phase 1 (Week 1 - Week 4): AWS Cloud Fundamentals & Core Laboratory Mastery** — Focusing on foundational cloud architecture theories (Compute, Networking, Storage, IAM Security, High Availability) alongside hands-on AWS laboratory exercises.
